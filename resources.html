Further reading

Free papers, books, courses, standards, reports and videos that take each part of the project further. Every module page ends with its own short list; here they are all together, to filter, search and cite.

About this list

  • Free to read

    Every item can be read online at no cost. Most open directly; the few marked “Free with registration” ask for a free account first.

  • Checked twice

    Each entry was found by a web search, then independently re-checked by a second search that confirmed its title, authors, year, address and free access.

  • Links can move

    Publishers reorganise their sites, so an address that worked when it was checked may break later. Open “Cite” on any card for the full reference, which is enough to find the work again.
