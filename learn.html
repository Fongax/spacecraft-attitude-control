    Shorter routes through the path

    The modules are written to be read in order, but each one names its prerequisites, so you can also follow a theme. Three suggestions:

    What a module page contains

    1. A purpose line, its phase in the lifecycle and the modules it builds on.
    2. “By the end you can…”: four to six objectives to check yourself against.
    3. Sections of explanation with equations, units and worked numbers, and interactive widgets. Every widget says what to try and what to notice.
    4. “Try it in the simulator”: links that open the full simulator on a matching scenario.
    5. “Further reading”: free papers, books, courses and reports that go deeper, from first reads to research, all gathered on the Resources page.
    6. Three questions to answer before you reveal the explanation.
    7. “Where this came from”: every subject whose material feeds the module, grouped by discipline, with how strong the link is and where the evidence comes from.
